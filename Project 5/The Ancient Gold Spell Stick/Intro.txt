=== THE ANCIENT GOLD SPELL STICK ===

An old, mysterious map has come into your hands. It shows where the
Ancient Gold Spell Stick was lost many years ago.

The map leads you to an abandoned ancient village. Nobody lives there
anymore. It is dark and quiet. There is an old cemetery, ruined buildings
and a large ancient castle. Fog covers the ground and you hear strange sounds.

Your backpack has three gold coins, a rope and a torch.
Use them wisely. Find the Ancient Gold Spell Stick!
