--- HOW TO PLAY ---
Type a command and press Enter. The menu is shown again after every command.

kartta   - look at the map (where you are and where you can go)
katso    - look around the room
liiku    - move to another room
ota      - collect the item in this room
reppu    - show your backpack
taika    - cast a spell
bell     - ring the bell
tallenna - save the game
ohje     - show these instructions again
lopeta   - quit the game

TIPS
- Dark places are hard to explore without a light.
- Ghosts do not like magic.
- The castle guard does not let anyone in for free.
- Some doors need more than one thing to open.

SAVING
Your progress is saved with your name. Next time, write the same name
and choose "yes" to continue your game.
